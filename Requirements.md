# WorldWideReport — Project Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

The WorldWideReport is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze worldwide business performance. The report provides an executive-level overview of sales and profitability and allows users to investigate profit performance in greater detail.

## 2. Project Objective

The primary objective of this report is to:

* Monitor overall business performance.
* Analyze sales and profitability.
* Identify profitable and underperforming areas.
* Compare performance across different business dimensions.
* Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 3. Business Requirements

### Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard should provide a high-level summary of business performance and allow users to quickly understand the overall results.

It should:

* Present the most important business KPIs.
* Provide an overview of sales and profit performance.
* Show trends and comparisons that help identify changes in performance.
* Highlight key areas of business performance.
* Provide interactive filtering for deeper analysis.

### Profit Analysis

The Profit Analysis page should provide a detailed view of profitability.

It should:

* Analyze profit performance.
* Identify high- and low-profit areas.
* Allow comparison between different business segments.
* Show trends in profit over time.
* Help identify factors contributing to profitability.
* Support interactive exploration through filters and visualizations.

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The report should provide relevant measures for evaluating business performance, including:

* **Total Sales** — Overall sales generated.
* **Total Profit** — Overall profit generated.
* **Total Quantity** — Total quantity of products sold.
* **Profit Margin** — Profit relative to sales.
* **Order/Transaction Count** — Number of business transactions, where available in the dataset.

## 5. Analysis Requirements

The report should allow users to analyze performance by available dimensions in the dataset, such as:

* Time/Date
* Country
* Region
* Product
* Category
* Other available business dimensions

The report should make it easy to identify:

* Top-performing areas.
* Underperforming areas.
* Sales and profit trends.
* Differences between regions and products.
* Changes in business performance over time.

## 6. Interactivity Requirements

The Power BI report should provide interactive features that allow users to:

* Filter the report using slicers.
* Cross-filter visuals.
* Drill into relevant information where applicable.
* Compare different categories or business segments.
* Navigate between report pages.

## 7. Report Pages

The current report consists of the following key pages:

### 1. Executive Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of overall business performance and key metrics.

### 2. Profit Analysis

Provides detailed analysis of profit and helps identify trends and performance differences.

## 8. Data Requirements

The report should use the provided WorldWideReport dataset and ensure that:

* Data is loaded correctly into Power BI.
* Required fields are available for analysis.
* Data types are appropriate.
* Calculated measures are accurate.
* Relationships between tables, where applicable, are correctly configured.
* Visuals accurately represent the underlying data.

## 9. Visualization Requirements

Visualizations should be:

* Clear and easy to understand.
* Consistent across report pages.
* Appropriate for the type of data being presented.
* Designed to support business decision-making.
* Interactive where appropriate.

The dashboard should maintain a consistent visual theme and provide clear titles, labels, and KPI descriptions.

## 10. User Requirements

The report is intended for users who need to quickly understand worldwide business performance and investigate profitability.

Users should be able to:

* Understand overall performance quickly.
* Identify important trends.
* Compare business performance across available dimensions.
* Investigate profit performance.
* Use filters to focus on specific areas of interest.

## 11. Expected Outcome

The completed WorldWideReport should provide an interactive and user-friendly Power BI solution that transforms the underlying business data into meaningful insights.

The report should help users:

* Understand overall business performance.
* Monitor sales and profitability.
* Identify opportunities and areas requiring attention.
* Support data-driven business decisions.
